Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Who We Are

AtriumX is a campus marketplace platform that lets students buy, sell, and trade with others in their residence and campus community. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, store, use, and protect your personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA), the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act 25 of 2002 (ECT Act), and other applicable laws of the Republic of South Africa.

For all privacy-related enquiries, contact us at: students@atriumx.co.za

2. What Information We Collect

We collect different information depending on how you use AtriumX.

When you create an account:

  • Email address and password (your password is handled by our authentication provider and is never visible to us in plain text)
  • Full name and residence/hall
  • An auto-generated avatar (your initials and an accent colour — we don't require a photo)

When you post a listing:

  • Title, description, price, and category
  • Photos or a video link you choose to upload
  • Your residence, and whether the price is negotiable

When you message another user:

  • The content of messages you send
  • The date and time messages are sent
  • Your account identifier, so messages can be matched to the correct conversation

When you rate a seller or report a listing:

  • Your star rating and any written comment you leave
  • The reason you give when reporting a listing

When you apply as a business or campus partner:

  • Business name, type, and description
  • Contact person's name, email address, and phone number
  • The package you select

You do not need an AtriumX account to submit a business or partner application.

Automatically, for all visitors:

  • We do not use advertising networks or analytics trackers
  • Your sign-in session is kept using your browser's local storage, not third-party tracking cookies
  • Our page fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may receive your device's IP address as part of that request
  • Our hosting and database provider, Supabase, may automatically log technical information such as IP address and request timestamps as part of operating and securing the platform

3. Why We Collect It

We collect this information to operate the AtriumX marketplace: to let students in a residence or campus community list, discover, and message each other about items; to calculate seller ratings; to review reported listings; to evaluate business and partner applications; and to keep the platform secure and functioning correctly.

We do not use your information for marketing. We do not sell your information to any third party. We do not use your information for any purpose beyond what is described in this policy.

4. What Other Users Can See

AtriumX is a marketplace by design, so some information is visible to other users, and in some cases to visitors who aren't signed in:

  • Public on the platform: your name, avatar, residence, average rating, number of listings, and any active listing you post
  • Visible to a specific user: the content of any conversation you have with them
  • Visible on your profile: ratings and comments other users have left about you

Your email address is used only for signing in and for account-related communication from AtriumX. It is not intended to be shown to other users anywhere in the app.

5. Who We Share It With

We rely on Supabase to host our database, authentication, and file storage — your information is processed on our behalf on their infrastructure. We don't share your information with advertisers, data brokers, or any other third party, and we don't currently use a payment processor. If that changes, we'll update this policy before it happens.

6. How Long We Keep It

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. Listings automatically stop being shown after their visibility period ends (3 to 30 days, depending on your plan), but the record of a listing, its messages, and any ratings tied to it may be kept for longer to resolve disputes, prevent abuse, or meet legal obligations. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time — see Section 10.

7. Local Storage and Cookies

AtriumX uses your browser's local storage to keep you signed in between visits. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. Clearing your browser's site data will sign you out.

8. Automated Decision-Making

AtriumX does not make any decision based solely on automated processing that would result in profiling or have a legal or similarly significant effect on you, as described in section 71 of POPIA. Moderation decisions, such as removing a reported listing or reviewing a business application, are made or confirmed by a human administrator.

9. Your Rights Under POPIA

The Protection of Personal Information Act gives you the following rights regarding your personal information held by AtriumX:

  • Right to access — you may request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Right to correction — you may request that inaccurate information be corrected
  • Right to deletion — you may request that your personal information be deleted from our records
  • Right to object — you may object to the processing of your personal information, including its use for a purpose you did not originally consent to

To exercise any of these rights, email us at students@atriumx.co.za. We will respond within a reasonable time and in any event within 30 days.

10. Data Security and Security Compromises

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the personal information we hold, including restricting administrator access, applying access-control rules at the database level, and using industry-standard infrastructure providers. No system can be guaranteed 100% secure, and AtriumX cannot accept liability for unauthorised access that occurs despite these reasonable measures.

In the event of a security compromise that has, on reasonable grounds, resulted in unauthorised access to your personal information, we will notify the Information Regulator and affected users as required by section 22 of POPIA. If you become aware of any security concern related to your information on this platform, contact us immediately at students@atriumx.co.za.
