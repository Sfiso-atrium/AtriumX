Safety Tips

AtriumX connects you with other students across your campus community — that's what makes it useful. Here's how to keep exchanges safe.

Last updated: August 2026

1. Meeting Up to Exchange an Item

  • Meet in a public, well-lit common area of the residence — a lobby, common room, or reception — rather than a private room, where possible
  • Bring a friend along, or let someone know where you're going and who you're meeting
  • Meet during the day if you can
  • Check the item in person before you pay — make sure it matches the listing and actually works

2. Paying for an Item

  • Prefer paying in person, at the point of exchange, over sending money upfront
  • Be cautious of any seller who pushes you to pay before you've seen the item or asks you to use an unusual payment method
  • AtriumX does not process payments for listings between students — any payment you make is arranged directly between you and the other user, and isn't protected or refunded by AtriumX

3. Keep Conversations on AtriumX

Chat through AtriumX's messaging rather than moving to a personal number or another app right away. It keeps a record of what was agreed, and makes it possible for us to look into a conversation if something goes wrong.

4. Trust Your Judgement

  • If a listing seems too good to be true, or a seller is unusually pushy, it's okay to walk away
  • You're never obligated to complete a sale just because you messaged someone about it
  • Check a seller's rating and past listings before agreeing to meet

Food listings

Items listed under the Food category aren't inspected or certified by AtriumX. Use your own judgement about freshness, allergens, and where it was prepared before buying.

5. If Something Feels Wrong

If a listing looks like a scam, or someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, don't meet up — report the listing instead. If you're ever in a situation you feel is unsafe, prioritise your own safety first and involve campus security or the relevant authorities before anything else.

Report a listing

Use the report option on any listing to flag it to an administrator, or email us directly.
