Safety Tips

AtriumX connects students with other students, businesses, and student-accommodation providers across participating campus communities. Here's how to use those spaces safely.

Last updated: September 2026

1. Meeting Up to Exchange an Item

  • Meet in a public, well-lit common area of the residence — a lobby, common room, or reception — rather than a private room, where possible
  • Bring a friend along, or let someone know where you're going and who you're meeting
  • Meet during the day if you can
  • Check the item in person before you pay — make sure it matches the listing and actually works

2. Paying for an Item

  • Prefer paying in person, at the point of exchange, over sending money upfront
  • Be cautious of any seller who pushes you to pay before you've seen the item or asks you to use an unusual payment method
  • AtriumX does not process student-to-student marketplace payments or student-to-accommodation rent payments — those arrangements are made directly with the other party and are not protected or refunded by AtriumX
  • PayFast is used for eligible AtriumX plan purchases by businesses and accommodation providers; that plan payment is separate from any sale, rent, deposit, or tenancy payment you may make to another user or provider

3. Keep Conversations on AtriumX

Chat through AtriumX's messaging rather than moving to a personal number or another app right away. It keeps a record of what was agreed, and makes it possible for us to look into a conversation if something goes wrong.

4. Trust Your Judgement

  • If a listing seems too good to be true, or a seller is unusually pushy, it's okay to walk away
  • You're never obligated to complete a sale just because you messaged someone about it
  • Check a seller's rating and past listings before agreeing to meet

Food listings

Items listed under the Food category aren't inspected or certified by AtriumX. Use your own judgement about freshness, allergens, and where it was prepared before buying.

5. Viewing Accommodation

  • Verify the residence address and relevant property details before travelling to view an accommodation listing
  • Ask the provider about rent, deposits, lease terms, included services, and availability before making any commitment
  • Do not send money merely because a listing, review, or message looks convincing; independently verify the provider and the property first
  • Where possible, view the property in person and take someone you trust with you

Accommodation and business content

AtriumX does not control what users or providers say about a business, business listing, accommodation provider, or accommodation property. Reviews, ratings, descriptions, messages, and replies are user-generated content. Read them critically and verify important claims yourself.

6. If Something Feels Wrong

If a listing looks like a scam, or someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, don't meet up — report the listing instead. If you're ever in a situation you feel is unsafe, prioritise your own safety first and involve campus security or the relevant authorities before anything else.

Report a listing

Use the report option on a marketplace, business, accommodation listing, or relevant conversation to flag it to an administrator, or email us directly. Reporting does not guarantee that content will be removed or that a particular action will be taken.

7. Study Group Chats

A Study Group chat can have several members, not just the one other person you'd be messaging in a regular trade conversation — keep that in mind when deciding what to share. Accommodation and business conversations can also involve people you do not know personally, so keep sensitive information to a minimum.

  • Only share images relevant to what you're studying — you don't need to share personal photos with a group of classmates
  • Be cautious about sharing personal contact details in a group chat; not everyone in it may be someone you know well
  • You can leave a Study Group at any time if someone in it makes you uncomfortable

Sharing images in groups

Once an image is shared in a group chat, everyone currently in that group can see it. Think about who's in the group before sharing anything you wouldn't want more widely seen.
