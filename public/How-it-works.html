How It Works

AtriumX is the campus economy, organised. Here's what happens from posting a listing to closing the deal, plus how events, accommodation, businesses, and student tools fit in.

01

Post a listing

Pick a category — Textbooks, Electronics, Clothing, Food, Services, Furniture, or Other — add a title, price, description, and photos (Ghost listings are text only), and choose a plan. Your plan decides how long the listing stays visible and how many photos you can add, from the free Ghost plan up to Unmissable.

02

It goes live in the Feed

Once a listing has completed the relevant posting and approval checks, it appears in the Feed for the campus community to browse. The marketplace is scoped to one university at a time, so listings from different universities are not mixed together.

03

A buyer finds it and reaches out

A student browsing the Feed clicks into a listing that catches their eye. If they're interested, they can message the seller directly through AtriumX where messaging is enabled — no need to leave the app or share a personal number.

04

You chat and sort out the details

Buyer and seller talk price, timing, and where to meet, all inside the AtriumX chat. Take a look at our Safety Tips before you meet up.

05

Meet up and exchange

Meet in a public spot in the residence, check the item, and complete the exchange in person. Payment is arranged directly between the two of you.

06

Leave a rating

Once you've bought from a seller, you can leave them a rating. Ratings build up on a seller's profile, so future buyers know who they're dealing with.

Business accounts

Businesses do not need a manual application-review stage. They choose a university when creating the account and move into the business experience after account creation.

  • Noticeboard — free, with access to 1 university
  • Featured — R350, with access to 2 universities
  • Campus Partner — R800, with access to 3 universities
  • The business university-access control lets the account move between its permitted university marketplaces. Each marketplace view stays within one university.
  • Ordinary business accounts use Discover as home and do not have My Space.

Accommodation

Accommodation has its own page rather than being buried in the normal marketplace. A provider is represented by one property listing for the whole property, including its buildings.

  • Accommodation prices are monthly.
  • Providers can add building information, room-sharing and pricing information, a description, photos, and amenities such as a gym, 24-hour study room, Wi-Fi, laundry, security, CCTV, parking, pool, backup power, common areas, cleaning services, and a shuttle.
  • Free — 1 university, 1 accommodation listing, up to 3 photos
  • Featured — R199/month — 2 universities, 1 accommodation listing, up to 12 photos, featured placement, and accommodation review replies
  • Premium — R399/month — 3 universities, 1 accommodation listing, up to 30 photos, premium featured placement, optional property video, and accommodation review replies
  • Accommodation accounts have their own home and do not use the normal Marketplace or Events area.
  • Students can message accommodation providers. Ordinary business accounts do not use accommodation messaging.
  • If a provider supplies a website, it can be opened from the accommodation card or details page. If no website is supplied, AtriumX indicates that no website was provided.

Events

  • Events are posted without a separate event payment/plan step for now.
  • An event can include a title, poster, category, future date and start time, location, description, and optional ticket price.
  • Students can browse the Events board, open event details, and interact with events from the event cards.

Beyond buying and selling

AtriumX has grown into more than a marketplace — it's also where you organise your student life:

  • My Space — keep your deadlines, class timetable, weekly schedule, and budget in one place, plus a built-in Focus Mode for timed study sessions
  • Study Groups — create a group with classmates, share an invite link to add them, and chat with shared deadlines, timetables, and schedules just for that group
  • Notebook — keep private notes and files behind a passcode that AtriumX does not store. If the passcode is lost, the existing notebook cannot be recovered and a new notebook must be created.
  • Dark Mode — switch the interface using the existing mode control.
  • Install it as an app — look for the Download button to add AtriumX to your phone or desktop for quicker access

Reviews, reports, and user content

  • Accommodation reviews are submitted by students. Free accommodation providers can read reviews, while Featured and Premium providers can reply.
  • Where reporting is available, reports are sent to the AtriumX administration area for review.
  • AtriumX has no control over what users say about businesses, sellers, or accommodation providers. Reviews, comments, descriptions, messages, and other user-submitted statements are the responsibility of the users who submit them, and AtriumX does not endorse or guarantee those statements.

A few other things worth knowing

  • Listings automatically stop showing once their plan's visibility window ends — you can renew from the listing's own page on plans that support it
  • Spot something that shouldn't be on the platform? You can report any listing directly from its page
  • Paid AtriumX plan upgrades are handled through PayFast. AtriumX does not use PayFast as the checkout for the underlying student-to-seller transaction.
