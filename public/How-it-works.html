How It Works

AtriumX has different flows for students, businesses, accommodation providers, and events. This page reflects the current product so you know what happens before you start.

01

Post a student marketplace listing

Choose the listing category, add the information requested by the form, then choose a student plan. Ghost is text-only and free; higher plans add photos, longer visibility, more active listings, and other listing features.

02

Browse one university marketplace at a time

The student marketplace is scoped to a university rather than mixing every campus together. Listings appear in the relevant university experience when they are eligible to be shown.

03

Message where the listing allows it

Students can open a listing and contact the seller through AtriumX where messaging is enabled by the plan and listing type.

04

Arrange the exchange yourself

AtriumX is not the checkout for the item. Buyer and seller agree on payment, timing, and where to meet. Read the Safety Tips before exchanging money or meeting someone.

05

Seller ratings stay separate from business and accommodation reviews

Marketplace ratings build the seller's profile. Businesses and accommodation use their own review systems, so the context of a review is clear.

Business listings

The main business onboarding flow is listing-first. A new business can choose a plan, fill in the business and listing details, select its university reach, and add the media allowed by that plan before creating the account password.

  • Noticeboard — Free / 7 days — 1 university, 1 active listing, up to 3 photos.
  • Featured — R199 / 30 days — up to 2 universities, up to 2 active listings, up to 5 photos, student messaging, and Sponsored visibility.
  • Campus Partner — R349 / 30 days — up to 3 universities, up to 3 active listings, up to 10 photos, 1 optional listing video, student messaging, business-review replies, pinned Business-tab visibility, and the Campus Partner badge.
  • After the listing details are ready, the business creates one password. Free Noticeboard listings can continue to publish; paid selections continue through PayFast without asking the business to rebuild the listing.
  • A business website can be shown from the business listing card. If no website was supplied, the card explains that no website is available rather than opening a dead link.
  • Ordinary business accounts do not use My Space.

Accommodation

Accommodation has its own discovery and review area. The browse page asks which university you want accommodation near before it loads properties; AtriumX does not show an all-universities accommodation feed.

  • A provider is represented by one accommodation listing for the whole property. One listing can include multiple buildings, room-sharing/pricing information, amenities, university reach, photos, and the provider website where supplied.
  • Free — 1 university, 1 accommodation listing, up to 3 photos.
  • Featured — R199 / 30 days — 2 universities, up to 12 photos, featured placement, and accommodation-review replies.
  • Premium — R399 / 30 days — 3 universities, up to 30 photos, premium featured placement, 1 optional property video, and accommodation-review replies.
  • The free outreach flow lets a provider submit the property first without creating an account. The submission is saved for review. If the provider chooses to create an account from that submission, the submitted accommodation name, university, email, address, and website are reused so only a password needs to be created.
  • If a paid accommodation plan is chosen before listing, the paid setup flow can create the login first, then continue into the matching accommodation form and payment flow.
  • Owners can edit their existing accommodation listing from their account. Students can message accommodation providers from the accommodation detail page.
  • Students can also review residences that do not yet have a public property listing by using the residence-review directory.

Accommodation reports and corrections

  • Accommodation reports are visible in the admin Reported area.
  • An administrator can select the exact listing field that needs correction and send the owner a 3-day correction notice.
  • The owner can use the focused Edit now action or the full listing editor. Updating the required field clears the correction deadline.
  • If the correction deadline expires without the required edit, the listing can stop appearing in the accommodation feed until it is corrected.

Reviews

  • Students can review businesses. Campus Partner businesses can reply to business reviews.
  • Students can review accommodation and residence entries. Free accommodation providers can read reviews; Featured and Premium providers can reply.
  • Reviews, replies, descriptions, and messages are user-generated content. AtriumX may moderate content under its rules but does not write or guarantee those statements.

Events

  • Events currently have no separate paid posting plan or event-payment step.
  • The event form collects the event information shown in the product and supports an optional poster/image.
  • Students can browse the Events board and open individual event details.

Student study and organisation tools

  • My Space — personal deadlines, timetable, schedule, budget, Pomodoro/focus sessions, watchlists, and Notebook access.
  • Focus Mode — timed study sessions connected to the same focus session used by My Space.
  • Study Toolkit — interactive periodic table, unit conversions, and formula sheets for Maths, Physics, and Chemistry.
  • QR Code tool — generate QR codes from text/links and scan QR codes using the device camera when permission is granted.
  • Study Groups — group chat plus shared deadlines, timetable, and schedule information.
  • Notebook — encrypted notes, drawings, and files protected by a passcode AtriumX does not store. Losing the passcode means the existing encrypted notebook cannot be recovered.
  • Dark Mode and install — the interface supports dark mode, and supported browsers can install AtriumX as a web app.

Notifications

  • Signed-in users can see unread notifications from the bell in the AtriumX navigation.
  • Supported browsers can also receive push notifications after the user explicitly enables them.
  • Notification actions route to the relevant area when a destination exists — for example a conversation, profile, listing, accommodation correction, My Space, or Study Group.

Payments and interruptions

  • Eligible paid student, business, and accommodation plans use PayFast.
  • Plan payment is separate from marketplace item payments, event ticket payments, rent, deposits, or tenancy arrangements between users and providers.
  • Business and accommodation paid flows keep the relevant draft through the payment handoff where the product supports resume/retry, so a cancelled or failed handoff does not require the user to rebuild the same listing from scratch.
