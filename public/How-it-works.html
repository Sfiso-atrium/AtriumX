How It Works

AtriumX is the campus economy, organised. Here's what happens from posting a listing to closing the deal.

01

Post a listing

Pick a category — Textbooks, Electronics, Clothing, Food, Services, Furniture, or Other — add a title, price, description, and photos, and choose a plan. Your plan decides how long the listing stays visible and how many photos you can add, from the free Ghost plan up to Unmissable.

02

It goes live in the Feed

Once your listing is reviewed and approved, it appears in the Feed for students in your residence and campus community to browse.

03

A buyer finds it and reaches out

A student browsing the Feed clicks into a listing that catches their eye. If they're interested, they message the seller directly, right there in the listing — no need to leave the app or share a personal number.

04

You chat and sort out the details

Buyer and seller talk price, timing, and where to meet, all inside the AtriumX chat. Take a look at our Safety Tips before you meet up.

05

Meet up and exchange

Meet in a public spot in the residence, check the item, and complete the exchange in person. Payment is arranged directly between the two of you.

06

Leave a rating

Once you've bought from a seller, you can leave them a rating. Ratings build up on a seller's profile, so future buyers know who they're dealing with.

A few other things worth knowing

  • Listings automatically stop showing once their plan's visibility window ends — you can renew from your profile on plans that support it
  • Spot something that shouldn't be on the platform? You can report any listing directly from its page
  • Businesses in the area can also advertise on AtriumX — see the Business page to apply
