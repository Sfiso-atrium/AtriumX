FAQ

Common questions about buying, selling, and using AtriumX.

Getting Started

What is AtriumX?

AtriumX is a campus marketplace — a place to buy, sell, and trade with other students in your residence and campus community.

Who can use it?

Anyone who's part of a residence or campus community covered by AtriumX. You'll need to register with your residence when you create an account.

Is it free to sign up?

Yes. Creating an account is free, and posting your first listing on the Ghost plan is free too.

Buying and Selling

How do I post a listing?

Choose a category, add a title, description, price, and photos, pick your residence, and select a plan. Once it's reviewed, it goes live in the Feed.

How long does my listing stay up?

Depends on your plan: Ghost gets 3 days, Visible gets 7, Loud gets 14, and Unmissable gets 30. See How It Works for the full breakdown.

Can I message a seller before buying?

Yes — click into any listing and message the seller directly through AtriumX's built-in chat. You don't need to share a personal number to ask a question.

How does payment work?

AtriumX doesn't process payments between students — you and the seller arrange payment directly, usually in person at the point of exchange. See our Safety Tips before you meet up.

What if I want to sell more than one item at a time?

How many active listings you can have depends on your plan — from 1 on Ghost up to 6 on Unmissable.

Trust and Safety

How do ratings work?

After you've bought from a seller, you can leave them a rating tied to that listing. Ratings show up on a seller's profile so other buyers know what to expect.

A listing looks like a scam — what do I do?

Don't meet up. Use the report option on the listing to flag it to an administrator, and check our Safety Tips for more on staying safe.

Is my personal information visible to other users?

Other users can see your name, avatar, residence, rating, and any active listings — not your email address. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.

For Businesses

Can businesses advertise on AtriumX?

Yes — local businesses can apply for the Noticeboard, Featured, or Campus Partner packages to reach students directly in their residences. See our Terms of Service for package details.

Account

Can I delete my account?

Yes, at any time. Email us and we'll take care of it — see our Privacy Policy for how we handle your data after that.
