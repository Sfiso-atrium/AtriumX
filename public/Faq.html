FAQ

Common questions about marketplace listings, businesses, accommodation, events, reviews, student tools, payments, and account access on AtriumX.

Getting Started

What is AtriumX?

AtriumX is a university-focused platform that brings together a student marketplace, campus events, accommodation discovery and reviews, business discovery, messaging, and student productivity tools.

Who can use it?

Students use the student experience for their university. Businesses use a separate business experience, and student-accommodation providers use the accommodation experience. Some browse pages are available while logged out. Most posting, messaging, review, and management actions require the appropriate account, although an accommodation provider can submit a guest accommodation listing before choosing whether to create an account.

Is it free to get started?

Yes. Student marketplace listings can start on Ghost, businesses can start on Noticeboard, and accommodation can start on the Free accommodation plan. Paid plans add more reach, media, visibility, or account features.

Student Marketplace

How do I post a student listing?

Choose a category, add the information requested by the listing form, choose your plan, and publish. The plan controls things such as visibility length, active-listing limits, photos, message limits, and other listing features.

What are the student marketplace plans?

Ghost is Free for 3 days with 1 active text-only listing and up to 3 messages per conversation. Visible is R29 for 7 days with 1 photo, 1 active listing, up to 10 messages per conversation, the Spotted badge, and renewal. Loud is R79 for 14 days with up to 2 photos, 2 active listings, unlimited messaging, boosted placement, and the Verified badge. Unmissable is R149 for 30 days with up to 3 photos, 3 active listings, top-of-category placement, unlimited messaging, and the Featured badge.

Can I message a seller before buying?

Yes, where messaging is enabled by the listing and plan. AtriumX chat is intended for arranging the transaction without requiring you to immediately share a personal phone number.

How does payment for an item work?

AtriumX does not process the payment for the item itself. Buyer and seller arrange that transaction directly. PayFast is used for eligible AtriumX plan purchases and upgrades, not as the checkout for the item being sold. See our Safety Tips before meeting or paying someone.

How many student listings can I have?

Ghost and Visible allow 1 active listing, Loud allows 2, and Unmissable allows 3.

Businesses

How does a new business join AtriumX?

The main business flow is listing-first: choose a business plan, add the business details, university reach, listing details, and allowed media, then create one password at the end so you can sign in and manage the business later. Noticeboard can publish after account creation; paid plans continue to PayFast while keeping the completed listing draft.

Does a business need manual approval before using AtriumX?

No. Ordinary business accounts do not have a separate manual application-review stage before account use.

What are the business plans?

Noticeboard is Free for 7 days, reaches 1 university, allows 1 active listing and up to 3 photos. Featured is R199 for 30 days, reaches up to 2 universities, allows 2 active listings and up to 5 photos, and enables student messaging. Campus Partner is R349 for 30 days, reaches up to 3 universities, allows 3 active listings, up to 10 photos and 1 optional listing video, enables messaging, allows replies to business reviews, and receives Campus Partner visibility features.

Can businesses add a website?

Yes. A business can provide a website when creating or managing its details. Business listing cards keep the website action visible; if no website was supplied, AtriumX tells the visitor that no website was provided instead of sending them to a dead link.

Can students review a business?

Yes. Student business reviews are separate from buyer-to-seller marketplace ratings. Students can leave a business review, and Campus Partner businesses can reply to those reviews.

Do business accounts have My Space?

No. My Space and the personal study tools are for student accounts. Ordinary business accounts use the business/Discover experience and manage their listings from their account.

Accommodation

How do I browse accommodation?

Choose the university you want accommodation near first. AtriumX then loads accommodation and residence reviews for that university only; there is no all-universities accommodation view.

Is accommodation part of the normal marketplace?

No. Accommodation has its own browsing, listing, review, messaging, and management experience.

How does an accommodation provider list a property?

A provider can submit the property details first without creating an account. The submission is saved for review. Creating an accommodation account afterward is optional; when it is created from that submission, AtriumX reuses the submitted accommodation name, university, email, property address, and website so the provider only needs to create the password. A provider account has one accommodation listing representing the whole property, including its buildings.

Can an accommodation provider edit the listing later?

Yes. Once the listing is linked to the provider account, the owner can open the accommodation editor from the profile or accommodation detail page and update the existing listing rather than creating another one.

What are the accommodation plans?

Free allows 1 university, 1 accommodation listing, and up to 3 photos. Featured is R199 for 30 days and allows 2 universities, up to 12 photos, featured placement, and replies to accommodation reviews. Premium is R399 for 30 days and allows 3 universities, up to 30 photos, premium featured placement, 1 optional property video, and replies to accommodation reviews.

What information can an accommodation listing contain?

The form supports the property name, building count, address information, university reach, description, amenities, room-sharing/pricing information, photos, and the provider website where supplied. Paid tiers can add the extra media and reach allowed by the selected plan.

Can I review a residence that has not listed on AtriumX?

Yes. The residence-review directory is designed to include student residence reviews even when the residence does not yet have a public accommodation listing. You must be signed in as a student to submit a review.

How do accommodation review replies work?

Students can submit accommodation reviews. Free providers can read reviews; Featured and Premium providers can reply. Replies appear with the review and remain the provider's own content.

What happens if accommodation information is reported?

Accommodation reports appear in the admin area. An administrator can ask the provider to correct a specific listing field and send a 3-day correction notice. Editing the required field clears the correction deadline; if the deadline expires without the required correction, the listing can be removed from the accommodation feed until it is corrected.

Can I visit the accommodation provider's website?

Yes, when one was supplied. The accommodation card and detail page provide the website action. If no website was provided, AtriumX clearly says so.

Events

What can an event contain?

Events can include the event information requested by the form, including a title, category, future date and time, location, description, optional ticket price, and an optional poster/image.

Do events have a paid posting plan?

No. Event posting currently has no separate plan or payment step.

My Space and Study Tools

What is My Space?

My Space is the private student planning area for deadlines, timetable, schedule, budget, focus/Pomodoro sessions, watchlists, and access to the private Notebook.

What are Study Groups?

Study Groups provide a shared group chat plus group deadlines, timetable, and schedule information. An invite link can take a student through sign-in and back into the group.

What is in the Study Toolkit?

The Toolkit includes an interactive periodic table, unit conversions, and formula sheets for Maths, Physics, and Chemistry. Formula sheets are reference tools and should still be checked against the requirements of your course.

What does the QR tool do?

Students can generate QR codes from text or links and scan QR codes with the device camera. The QR generation and scanning logic runs in the browser; camera access still depends on the permission you give your browser.

What happens if I forget my Notebook passcode?

The existing encrypted notebook cannot be recovered because AtriumX does not store the passcode or an unencrypted notebook key. Resetting the Notebook removes the existing encrypted notes and files and lets you create a new passcode.

Can I install AtriumX as an app?

Yes. Where the browser supports it, use the Download/install option. On iPhone, installation is handled through Safari's Share menu rather than the same one-tap install flow used by many Android and desktop browsers.

Can I use Dark Mode?

Yes. Use the mode control in the interface to switch between the available light and dark appearance.

Where do notifications appear?

Signed-in users receive unread notifications in the bell in the AtriumX navigation. Where browser push is supported and you choose to enable it, AtriumX can also deliver supported notifications through your browser's push service. Opening a notification takes you to the relevant part of AtriumX where that notification type supports a destination.

Trust, Reports, and Reviews

How do seller ratings work?

Marketplace ratings are tied to the marketplace transaction flow and build up on the seller profile. Business reviews and accommodation reviews are separate review systems.

A listing looks suspicious — what do I do?

Do not proceed with the transaction or viewing if you feel unsafe. Use the available report action and see our Safety Tips.

How do reports work?

Where reporting is available, the report is sent to the AtriumX admin area for review. A report does not guarantee a particular outcome. Accommodation reports can also be used to request a specific correction from the provider.

Does AtriumX control what users say in reviews or listings?

No. Listings, messages, ratings, reviews, replies, descriptions, and other user-submitted statements come from the people who submit them. AtriumX may moderate content under its rules, but does not endorse or guarantee the truth of user-generated statements.

Is my email public?

No. Email addresses are used for account access and account-related communication and are not intended to be displayed as public profile information. See the Privacy Policy for details about other information that may be visible.

Accounts and Payments

How are paid AtriumX plans paid for?

Paid student marketplace, business, and accommodation plan purchases are handled through PayFast. Payment for an AtriumX plan is separate from any item sale, event ticket, rent, deposit, or tenancy payment arranged with another person or provider.

What happens if a paid business flow is interrupted?

The completed business listing draft is kept through the paid-plan handoff so the business does not have to re-enter the same listing details simply because payment was cancelled or retried.

Can I delete my account?

Yes. Contact us to request deletion. The Privacy Policy explains how account information and records are handled.
