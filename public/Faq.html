FAQ

Common questions about buying, selling, events, accommodation, student tools, and using AtriumX.

Getting Started

What is AtriumX?

AtriumX is a university-focused platform that brings together a marketplace, campus events, accommodation, messaging, and student productivity tools in one place.

Who can use it?

Students can use the student experience for their university. Businesses can create business accounts and select a university. Businesses can also identify themselves as accommodation providers and use the separate accommodation experience.

Is it free to sign up?

Yes. Creating an account is free, and posting your first student marketplace listing on the Ghost plan is free too. Ordinary businesses start on the free Noticeboard plan, and accommodation providers start on the free accommodation plan.

Buying and Selling

How do I post a listing?

Choose a category, add a title, description, price, and photos where your plan allows, choose the university reach available to your account, and select a plan. Listings then follow the relevant posting and approval checks before they appear in the Feed.

How long does my listing stay up?

For student marketplace plans, Ghost gets 3 days, Visible gets 7, Loud gets 14, and Unmissable gets 30. See How It Works for more about the other plan families.

Can I message a seller before buying?

Yes — click into a listing and message the seller directly through AtriumX where messaging is enabled.

How does payment work?

AtriumX doesn't process payments between students and sellers — you and the seller arrange payment directly, usually in person at the point of exchange. PayFast is used for AtriumX plan payments and upgrades, not as the checkout for the underlying marketplace transaction. See our Safety Tips before you meet up.

What if I want to sell more than one item at a time?

How many active listings you can have depends on your student plan — from 1 on Ghost and Visible up to 3 on Unmissable.

Businesses

Can businesses advertise on AtriumX?

Yes — local businesses can create business accounts and choose a university without a manual application-review stage. The available packages are Noticeboard, Featured, and Campus Partner.

How many universities can a business reach?

Noticeboard allows 1 university, Featured allows 2, and Campus Partner allows 3. The business can switch between its permitted university marketplaces, but one marketplace view never mixes listings from different universities.

What are the business plan prices?

Noticeboard is free, Featured is R350, and Campus Partner is R800.

Do business accounts have My Space?

No. Discover is the home experience for ordinary business accounts.

Accommodation

Is accommodation part of the normal marketplace?

No. Accommodation has its own page so properties have dedicated visibility rather than being buried among ordinary marketplace listings. Accommodation accounts do not use the normal Marketplace or Events experience.

How is an accommodation property listed?

One accommodation provider is represented by one property listing for the whole property. That listing can cover multiple buildings and include building information, room-sharing and pricing information, amenities, photos, and a property description.

Are accommodation prices monthly?

Yes. Accommodation pricing is monthly, with room pricing available by sharing type where the provider supplies it.

What are the accommodation plans?

Free allows 1 university, 1 accommodation listing, and up to 3 photos. Featured costs R199/month and allows 2 universities, 1 listing, up to 12 photos, featured placement, and replies to accommodation reviews. Premium costs R399/month and allows 3 universities, 1 listing, up to 30 photos, premium featured placement, optional property video, and replies to accommodation reviews.

Can an accommodation provider add amenities?

Yes. The form supports amenities such as a gym, 24-hour study room, Wi-Fi, laundry, security, CCTV, parking, pool, backup power, common areas, cleaning services, and a shuttle, along with other amenities the provider supplies.

Can I visit the accommodation provider's website?

Yes, when the provider has supplied a website. The accommodation card and detail page provide a way to visit it. If no website was provided, AtriumX indicates that no website was supplied.

Can I message an accommodation provider?

Students can message accommodation providers through the accommodation detail page. Ordinary business accounts do not use accommodation messaging.

How do accommodation reviews work?

Students can submit accommodation reviews. Free accommodation providers can read reviews, while Featured and Premium providers can reply to reviews.

Events

What can an event contain?

Events can include a title, poster, category, future date and start time, location, description, and an optional ticket price. Event cards also support likes and event details.

Do events have a paid posting plan?

No. Event posting currently has no separate plan or payment step.

My Space and Study Groups

What is My Space?

My Space is your personal planner inside AtriumX — deadlines, your class timetable, weekly schedule, a budget tracker, and a Focus Mode for timed study sessions. It's private to you.

What are Study Groups?

A Study Group is a shared space for you and your classmates — group chat, plus deadlines, timetable, and schedule information for that group, separate from your own personal ones in My Space.

How do I join a Study Group?

Someone in the group shares an invite link with you. Opening it takes you to sign in (or register, if you're new), then drops you straight into that group.

Can I install AtriumX as an app?

Yes — look for the Download button. On Android and most desktop browsers it installs in one tap. On iPhone, Safari doesn't allow one-tap installs, so you'll add it via the Share button instead.

What happens if I forget my Notebook passcode?

The existing notebook cannot be recovered. AtriumX does not store the passcode or an unencrypted copy of the notebook key. You must create a new notebook, which permanently removes the existing encrypted notebook.

Can I use Dark Mode?

Yes. Use the existing mode control in the interface to switch to Dark Mode.

Trust and Safety

How do ratings work?

After you've bought from a seller, you can leave them a rating tied to that listing. Ratings show up on a seller's profile so other buyers know what to expect.

A listing looks like a scam — what do I do?

Don't meet up. Use the report option on the listing to flag it to an administrator, and check our Safety Tips for more on staying safe.

How do I report something?

Where the report option is available, use it on the relevant listing or content. Reports are sent to the AtriumX administration area for review.

Does AtriumX control what users say about businesses or accommodation?

No. AtriumX does not write, control, guarantee, or endorse statements made by users about businesses, sellers, or accommodation providers. Reviews, comments, descriptions, messages, and other user-submitted statements come from the users who submit them.

Is my personal information visible to other users?

Other users can see the profile and listing information the platform makes available. Email addresses are not displayed as public profile information. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.

Account and Payments

How are paid AtriumX plans paid for?

Paid plan upgrades are handled through PayFast. This covers the paid marketplace, business, and accommodation plan flows. It does not make AtriumX the checkout for the underlying student-to-seller transaction.

Can I delete my account?

Yes, at any time. Email us and we'll take care of it — see our Privacy Policy for how we handle your data after that.
